# Remotion Prompt Bank

## Prompt: Depth

Use when a graphic feels flat and you want it to look like a real 3D space.

---

**Make it 2.5D with real depth: a perspective camera moving through the scene, depth of field (blurry foreground, sharp subject, soft background), and parallax layers.**

---

What each part does:
- **Perspective camera:** things get smaller as they get further away, and the camera actually flies through the scene instead of sliding a flat picture around.
- **Depth of field:** blurry things close to the lens and soft things far away. This is the biggest reason it reads as 3D.
- **Parallax layers:** foreground, midground and background move at different speeds when the camera moves.

Short version: **"perspective, depth of field, parallax."**
