# Brief: 3 tips for better motion graphics with AI

## What Remotion is (the simple version)

Remotion is a free tool that turns code into videos.

Normally, motion graphics (text flying in, cards sliding around, zooms, animated charts) get made by hand in a program like After Effects, which takes hours and real skill.

With Remotion, you don't do any of that by hand. You tell an AI like Claude or Codex what you want in plain English, for example: "make a card slide in from the left, zoom into the price, then follow it as it moves across the screen." The AI writes the code, and Remotion turns that code into a finished video file (an MP4).

So you don't need to know how to code or how to animate. You just need to be good at describing what you want. That's what this video is about.

## The 3 tips

### Tip 1: Pick one style and turn it into a skill
A "skill" is basically a saved instruction sheet you give the AI. You write down your look once (colors, fonts, background, how things move) and save it. From then on, every video comes out in that same style without you explaining it again. This is why all my videos look like they belong together.

### Tip 2: Describe the movement, not just what's on screen
If you only tell the AI what to show, you get boring videos where things fade in and just sit there. You have to tell it how things should move. For example: "smooth zoom-ins," "the camera follows the card as it moves," "one continuous scene, no hard cuts." That one change makes it look like a real editor made it.

### Tip 3: Give it references
Don't make the AI guess what "good" looks like. Show it. Before it builds anything, I give it examples:
- A video you like, so it can copy the pacing and the way things move
- Real screenshots of the app you're showing, so it doesn't make up fake buttons
- The official logos, instead of letting it draw its own

When the AI works from memory, it gets things close but slightly wrong, and people notice right away. With references, it matches what you actually want, and the video looks like the real product instead of AI.
